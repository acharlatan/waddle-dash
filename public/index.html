LET'S GET STARTED ✦

Bring your data in.

A little CSV can tell a big story.

A report Waddle can investigate

Choose a dated performance CSV with one campaign or channel per day, a date, a channel (or one you assign), and at least one numeric metric. Campaign names help with investigations. Contact or deal lists are not automatically performance reports.

For a later report, review its source and goal here, then confirm its channel, currency and definitions in the next step.

Review the file before importing. Open the full guide →

Your header, mappings, currency and definitions are kept until you choose another valid file.
✧

Your data stays yours. Your file is processed in your browser. Active data clears on refresh or leaving; Time Machine snapshots are saved only if you opt in.

▤

1. Choose the header

First choose the record containing your column names. Records count non-empty CSV records, including quoted multiline fields.

Changing the header resets column mappings to new suggestions. Your currency, source and conversion selections remain.

2. Match your columns

Map a date and at least one numeric metric, then map a channel or assign one below.

Optional if you mapped a Channel column. Choose the actual source or campaign type; not all Google Ads campaigns are Search.
This applies to the whole mapped column. Confirm that every row and comparison uses the same definition; Waddle cannot verify it from the file.
Choose a meaning if a value column is mapped. Platform-reported conversion value is not verified revenue or profit.
Select the file’s currency. A bare $ could mean USD, CAD, or AUD. Waddle does not convert currencies and cannot verify amounts without clear currency markers.

3. Review before importing

Check the parsed records, warnings and available analyses, then confirm below.
